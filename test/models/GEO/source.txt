GEO / Videoscape test fixtures
------------------------------
All meshes in this directory are original, minimal fixtures authored for
Assimp GEO importer unit tests (ZsoltTech / Arris, 2026). They are intended
to be redistributed with Assimp under the same terms as the rest of the
test/models tree.

They intentionally cover the Videoscape flavours used by the importer:
  gray_plane.3dg          — 3DG1 face-colored quad (hex color)
  colored_vertices.gour   — GOUR vertex-colored quad (BGR hex: R/G/B/W)
  colored_vertices.geo    — same GOUR payload, .geo extension
  box.3dg                 — 3DG1 palette-colored box (6 quads)
  pyramid.geo             — 3DG1 palette-colored pyramid
  two_lamps.3dg           — 3DG2 lights-only fixture (point + spot)

Hex BGR vs RGB probes (Videoscape stores hex as BGR per Bourke):
  hex_bgr_probe.gour      — GOUR quad, tokens chosen so CORRECT BGR decode is
                            TL red (0x0000ff), TR green (0x00ff00),
                            BR blue (0xff0000), BL white (0xffffff).
                            Wrong RGB decode swaps TL/BR to blue/red.
  hex_bgr_probe.3dg       — 3DG1 three side-by-side quads:
                            left 0x0000ff, mid 0x00ff00, right 0xff0000.
                            Correct BGR: red | green | blue.
                            Wrong RGB:   blue | green | red.
  bourke_gour_example.gour — Paul Bourke GOUR example (retyped from
                            https://paulbourke.net/dataformats/geo/).
                            First vertex 0x0000ff must be red under BGR.
  hex_top_blue_bot_red.3dg — 3DG1 two strips: top 0xff0000 (blue), bottom
                            0x0000ff (red) under BGR; Y-up screenshot check.
  hex_top_blue_bot_red.gour — same tokens as GOUR vertex colors.

These are NOT copies of third-party binary sample packs (ewhac, X GB, etc.).
Bourke's example above is the documented ASCII sample from his format page;
other meshes are original fixtures. Local third-party packs stay under
~/3D-Objekte/ and are not shipped in this repo.
